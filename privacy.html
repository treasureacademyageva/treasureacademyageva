Privacy Policy

How Treasure Academy, Ageva handles information shared through its website and verified contact channels. Last updated 21 October 2026.

Home›Family Help›Privacy Policy
Contact the schoolSearch this site

1. Who we are

Treasure Academy, Ageva is a Creche, Nursery and Primary school established in 2016 in Ageva, Okene, Kogi State, Nigeria. It is operated by Treasure Academy Ageva Limited, an incorporated company registered in Nigeria (RC-9634403). This policy applies to the public website and to records received and kept by the school office.

2. Information we collect

The public website does not operate a pupil database or accept portal passwords, results, health records or payment claims. When you choose a contact, admissions, careers or support action, the site opens the verified school WhatsApp, telephone or email service and you decide what to send there.

The website may remember non-sensitive preferences on your own device, such as light/dark mode and text size. A service worker may cache public pages and brand assets for basic offline access. These device copies are not sent to the school and can be cleared in your browser settings.

There is currently no advertising tracker or website analytics service enabled.

3. Information received by the school

The school office may collect pupil, parent, admission, attendance, result, fee and safeguarding information through its verified office processes. We use it only to operate the school, contact families, teach and assess pupils, protect children and meet legal or regulatory duties.

WhatsApp, email, telephone and Google Maps are separate services with their own privacy practices. The website opens them only when you choose the relevant link. Avoid sending passwords, PINs or full medical records through ordinary chat.

4. How we store and protect school records

Pupil and family records received by the school are kept through the school office and are limited to the Headmistress and authorised staff who need them for their work. The retired browser-only portal is not used as a production record system. Records are retained only for a necessary school, safeguarding or legal purpose and are disposed of safely when no longer required.

We never sell personal information. We share it only with appropriate authority, consent or a safeguarding/legal requirement.

5. Children's privacy

Children's passwords, candidate numbers, individual marks, dates of birth, phone numbers and medical details are not published through the public website. A child photograph, name or achievement should appear publicly only after the school has confirmed the appropriate parent or guardian authority. A parent or guardian may ask the school to remove an identifiable photograph or profile.

6. Your rights

As a parent or guardian you can ask us to:

  • See the information we hold about you and your child;
  • Correct anything that is wrong or out of date;
  • Delete information we no longer need to keep;
  • Stop using your child's photograph.

To make any of these requests, or if you have a concern about how we handle your data, please contact the school (see below).

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be announced on the school notice board and this page.
