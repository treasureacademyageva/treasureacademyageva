Privacy Policy

How Treasure Academy handles website preferences, performance measurement, requests, parent–pupil linking and school records. Last updated 23 October 2026.

Home›Family Help›Privacy Policy
Contact the schoolSearch this site

1. Who we are

Treasure Academy, Ageva is a Pre-Nursery, Nursery and Primary school established in 2016 in Ageva, Okene, Kogi State, Nigeria. It is operated by Treasure Academy Ageva Limited, an incorporated company registered in Nigeria (RC-9634403). This policy applies to the public website and to records received and kept by the school office.

2. Information we collect

The public Request & Verification Centre records the request type, your name, Nigerian phone number, optional email and class, subject, message, source, status and school follow-up notes. After database creation it issues a public reference and a private tracking code. Only a one-way hash of that private code is retained, so the readable code cannot be recovered.

The public website does not accept portal passwords, result entries, full medical files, bank-card details or payment claims. A request reference confirms receipt only; it does not approve admission, verify payment or promise an outcome. Authorised staff may register a phone, generic WhatsApp or office conversation in the same system and issue both tracking values privately.

The website may keep non-sensitive preferences on your device, such as light or dark mode. It also keeps a short list of broad action categories during the current tab session—for example, “opened fees” or “search category: admissions.” It never stores form values or raw search words in those local diagnostics.

A service worker may cache public pages and brand assets for basic offline access. You can clear these copies in your browser settings.

Vercel Speed Insights measures anonymous performance signals: route, network class, browser, device type, operating system, country, Web Vitals and the page element involved.

The measurement is not tied to an individual or IP address. It does not receive form content or raw search words, and it does not load when the browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. There is no advertising tracker or session recording.

3. Information received by the school

The school office may collect pupil, parent, admission, attendance, result, fee and safeguarding information through its verified office processes. We use it only to operate the school, contact families, teach and assess pupils, protect children and meet legal or regulatory duties.

For parent access, the portal may use the pupil’s typed register name, selected class, the authenticated parent account and a school-issued one-time linking code. It never displays a class list to the parent. Only a SHA-256 hash of a pupil-linking or second-parent invitation code is stored. A successful code creates a recorded parent–pupil relationship; later access uses the parent’s ordinary phone-and-password sign-in.

A pupil may have no more than two active parent or guardian accounts. A second-parent invitation is separate from the original pupil code, bound to the intended Nigerian phone number and consumed after use. New-pupil applications remain separate from existing-pupil linking, payments and final admission decisions.

WhatsApp, email, telephone and Google Maps are separate services with their own privacy practices. WhatsApp remains an optional generic contact channel, not the endpoint for a structured website request. Avoid sending passwords, PINs or full medical records through ordinary chat.

Phone verification processor: when Treasure Academy enables automated WhatsApp authentication delivery, Paragon Hub acts only as the school’s separated technical processor for the registered phone number, short-lived one-time code, expiry and delivery status. That relay is not sent a pupil or parent name, class, result, admission record, payment, password or school-database credential. Treasure Academy Ageva Limited remains the publisher and data controller.

4. How we store and protect school records

The school office limits pupil and family records to the Headmistress and authorised staff who need them for their work.

  • The secure portal is a separate authenticated record system and is activated only after preservation, migration and access checks.
  • Records are kept only for a necessary school, safeguarding or legal purpose.
  • Records are disposed of safely when no longer required.

We never sell personal information. We share it only with appropriate authority, consent or a safeguarding or legal requirement.

5. Children's privacy

The public website does not publish a child’s password, candidate number, individual marks, date of birth, phone number or medical details.

  • A child’s photograph, name or achievement requires appropriate parent or guardian authority.
  • A parent or guardian may ask the school to remove an identifiable photograph or profile.

6. Your rights

As a parent or guardian you can ask us to:

  • See the information we hold about you and your child;
  • Correct anything that is wrong or out of date;
  • Delete information we no longer need to keep;
  • Stop using your child's photograph.

To make any of these requests, or if you have a concern about how we handle your data, please contact the school (see below).

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be announced on the school notice board and this page.
