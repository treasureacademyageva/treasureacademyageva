Consent-reviewed Graduate Wall

Approved graduate celebrations only

This is not a pupil directory or attendance lookup. It can display only the minimum name, graduation session and optional message that the school approved from a recorded consent review. Candidate numbers, results, contacts, class histories and finances remain private.

Request your own school record

A former pupil, parent or authorised guardian can ask the office what identification or authority is required. The school will verify the request privately before discussing any record.

Share an achievement update

Former pupils may contact the school with an update. If the person is under 18, a parent or guardian should make the request. Do not send exam numbers, results, home addresses or other sensitive records through the public website.

The school must verify the story and appropriate publication permission before posting a name, image or quote.
