Quick Answers

What Families Usually Ask First

Clear, current guidance from the school website.

Where is Treasure Academy?

Opposite Morak Pure-Water Factory, Ageva, Okene, Kogi State, Nigeria. Call before visiting, especially during holidays.

Which classes does the school offer?

Pre-Nursery, Nursery 1–2, Primary 1–4 and Primary 6. The office confirms current class availability and placement guidance.

How do I start an admission enquiry?

Use the restricted applicant route or the website Request Centre. Confirm the current form, documents and class availability; payment details appear only on an official invoice.

Can I check results through the website?

Parents with a school-issued account can use the secure portal to view published results for linked children. If portal access has not yet been activated for your family, contact the verified school office.
