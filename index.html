Quick Answers

What Families Usually Ask First

Clear, current guidance from the school website.

Where is Treasure Academy?

Opposite Morak Pure-Water Factory, Ageva, Okene, Kogi State, Nigeria. Call before visiting, especially during holidays.

Which classes does the school offer?

Creche, Pre-Nursery, Nursery 1–2 and Primary 1–6. The office confirms current class availability and placement guidance.

How do I start an admission enquiry?

Use the verified Call line or WhatsApp 09063932487. Confirm the current form, documents, class availability and bank details before paying.

Can I check results through the website?

No. The browser-only portal has been retired while a secure shared service is prepared. Contact the school office for parent records and support.
