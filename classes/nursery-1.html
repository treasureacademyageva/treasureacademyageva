What children build in Nursery 1

Nursery 1 builds reading and writing foundations with phonics, handwriting practice and simple numeracy.

Class routines and materials may change during the session. Parents can arrange a visit or ask the school for the current class plan.

Learning highlights

  • Phonics and reading
  • Handwriting
  • Simple counting
  • Colouring and craft
