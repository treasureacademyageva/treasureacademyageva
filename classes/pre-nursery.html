What children build in Pre-Nursery

Pre-Nursery blends play with early learning as children begin to recognise letters, numbers, colours and classroom routines.

This page gives families a clear introduction to Pre-Nursery. The school office confirms individual placement after considering the child’s previous schooling and the current class availability.

The learning highlights are a public overview rather than a fixed weekly timetable. Teachers may adjust the order and pace of classroom work to support the pupils in the class while keeping the stage goals clear.

Learning highlights

  • Letter and number play
  • Painting and building
  • Rhymes and stories
  • Table manners and sharing

How families can help

Read and talk together, encourage a calm daily routine and share any useful information about the child’s earlier learning with the school. Ask the teacher or office when guidance is unclear rather than relying on an older class list or fee notice.

Before applying

Confirm the child’s placement, available places, current fee schedule and required documents with the school office. An enquiry is separate from an application, and an application remains subject to an authorised admission decision.

Current books and materials

The current Pre-Nursery book package is ₦12,300. Required writing materials are 6 pencils, 2 big erasers, 3 razor blades and 1 packet of colour pencils. Writing-material prices are not included.

The current new-pupil admission and book total is ₦38,300. Families may buy the required materials elsewhere. After promotion, use the list for the pupil’s new class.
