A Brief History

began in 2016 through the burning desire in the heart of our founder, Shaibu Sidikat Ruth, a mother and trained teacher, to give children of Ageva a school that feels like home and teaches like the very best.

We started small: just a handful of pupils and a few dedicated teachers in a rented apartment. But our love was big! Word spread quickly about the school where children run to class with smiles, and within our first three years we moved to our own permanent site.

Today, we are a full Creche, Nursery and Primary school — our classrooms are filled up with happy pupils guided by qualified, loving teachers — with a computer room, playground and — our newest pride — an online portal where parents check results from their phones.

Through it all, one thing never changed: every child can excel — our work is to give them the foundation to do so.

Our Mission

Building an effective and efficient future leader: raising confident, brilliant and well-mannered children through strong academics and morals.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted school in Okene, where every graduate shines in secondary school and beyond.
