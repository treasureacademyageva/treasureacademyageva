A Brief History

Treasure Academy was founded in 2016 by Shaibu Sidikat Ruth to serve children and families in Ageva, Okene. The school began with a clear purpose: to help children grow in knowledge, character and confidence.

The school offers Pre-Nursery, Nursery 1–2, Primary 1–4 and Primary 6. Its stated focus is to build strong academic foundations, good character and readiness for each child's next stage.

Families are encouraged to visit the school and ask the office to confirm current facilities, class arrangements and availability.

Our Mission

To help children grow in knowledge, character and confidence through strong academics, clear guidance and good morals.

Our Vision

To nurture confident, capable and well-mannered children who are ready for the next stage of their education.

Our Values

Discipline · Excellence · Respect · Integrity
