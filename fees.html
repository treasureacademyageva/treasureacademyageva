Stock requests and payment

If a school-supplied item is unavailable, create a supplies request so the school knows it is needed. A request is not an order, stock promise or payment instruction. Advance payment may be accepted only after the school issues an official order or invoice; payment does not mean an item is ready for collection.

  1. Linked parents should check the pupil, class and current charge in the secure portal or official invoice.
  2. Pay only by approved bank transfer shown in the portal or official invoice, or at the school’s physical office POS.
  3. Wait for authorised office verification. The official receipt appears only afterwards.

There is no public online card form, automatic admission from payment or WhatsApp payment-evidence workflow.
