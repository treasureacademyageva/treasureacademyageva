School Anthem

Treasure Academy, our dear school, Where wisdom shines and hearts are true. In knowledge, character and fear of God, We grow together, strong and true. Our God is able! Our God is able! We rise in learning, we shine as one. Treasure Academy, lead us on — To serve our land and God alone!

School Creed

I am a Treasure pupil. I choose honesty over lies, hard work over shortcuts, and kindness over cruelty. I respect my elders, care for my friends, and keep my school clean. With God, all things are able — I will learn today and lead tomorrow.

School Pledge

I pledge to Treasure Academy, to be honest in my words, diligent in my work, and faithful in my duties, so help me God.

School Motto

“Our God is able.”
