    Usability & accessibility audit

    Targets shared navigation, buttons, forms, focus, labels, errors, text alternatives, image dimensions, readable mobile typography and one- or two-action discovery.

    Keyboard check

    • Start with the skip link.
    • Tab through the header in visual order.
    • Confirm every focus ring stays visible.
    • Open and close FAQ/details controls.
    • Trigger a form error and verify focus moves to it.

    Mobile check

    • 320px viewport without horizontal scroll.
    • Readable text without pinch zoom.
    • Five predictable bottom actions.
    • Tap targets at least 44px.
    • No focused control hidden behind the dock.

    Manual evidence still needed

    • Whether alt text accurately describes approved media.
    • Contrast over photos and gradients.
    • Screen-reader announcement order.
    • 200% and 400% zoom usability.
    • Real parent/staff task testing.

    Release controls

    What must remain true

    Accessibility, analytics and brand audits support review; they do not authorize fabricated content, insecure portals or unverified payment functionality.

    Content integrity

    • Owner supplies real school facts and media.
    • Do not describe unverified images as authentic.
    • Add social links only after profile ownership is confirmed.
    • Keep restoration and provenance records.

    Admissions and payment

    • Apply remains an enquiry and offline school process.
    • No online checkout or card collection.
    • Use bank transfer only after office confirmation.
    • Never generate a receipt or success claim in the browser.

    Accessibility

    • Target WCAG 2.2 AA components.
    • Run automated and manual keyboard checks.
    • Do not claim ADA/WCAG certification from automation alone.
    • Test with disabled users where possible.

    Privacy and safety

    • No pupil database or behaviour profiles.
    • No names, form values or raw searches in analytics.
    • No persistent journey ID or session replay.
    • Voluntary reports require visitor review and Send.
