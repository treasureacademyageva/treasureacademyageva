Public Website Quality and CMS Plan

This noindex page summarises the code-side operating model. It contains no credentials and is not the school-record portal.

Production posture

Static brochure and verified contacts

  • No public pupil database or browser authentication.
  • No browser-generated payment receipt.
  • Operational enquiries open the verified school office channel.
  • Retired portal routes redirect to support.

SEO and AEO

  • Canonical host: www.treasureacademyageva.com
  • Unique metadata, class URLs and published-news URL.
  • School/WebSite, Breadcrumb and NewsArticle JSON-LD.
  • Indexable sitemap excludes utilities and retired campaigns.
  • No fake review schema, AI-only markup or invented facts.

CMS direction

Managed conventional WordPress is the proposed owner-friendly public CMS. Keep pupil records and payments in a separate authenticated system.

Repository plan: docs/CMS-SEO-AEO-STRATEGY-2026.md
Starter: cms/wordpress-migration/

Content workflow

Draft → factual review → privacy/safeguarding review → owner approval → publish → scheduled review → archive.

Operational content needs a source, owner, last-reviewed date and expiry/review date.
