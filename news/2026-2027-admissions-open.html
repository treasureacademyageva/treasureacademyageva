2026/2027 Admissions Are Open

Published 15 September 2026 · Updated 21 October 2026

Treasure Academy, Ageva is receiving admission enquiries from Pre-Nursery to Primary 6 for the 2026/2027 academic session.

How to start safely

  1. Contact the school through the verified WhatsApp number or call line.
  2. Ask the office to confirm class availability, the current form fee and required documents.
  3. Confirm the current school bank account before making any transfer.
  4. Submit the official documents and visit the school when instructed.

The retired browser-only application form does not accept or store applications. An application is received only when the school office confirms it through the official process.

What to bring

The office will confirm the list for your child's class. It may include a birth certificate copy, passport photographs, a previous report card for a transfer pupil and an immunisation record for Pre-Nursery or Nursery.

Ask the Admissions Office

Do not transfer to an account that has not been confirmed through the verified contacts.
