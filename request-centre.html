One place for school requests

The success screen appears only after the school saves the request and creates its reference. A WhatsApp draft is not treated as proof of delivery.

  • Keep the reference and private tracking code together.
  • Do not include passwords, bank card details, medical files or unnecessary child information.
  • A request reference is not an admission, payment or publication decision.

Need ordinary contact?

Call +234 814 194 3478 or use generic WhatsApp 09063932487. Staff can register an off-site conversation and give you a website reference.

For an emergency or immediate safeguarding risk, use the appropriate emergency service and contact the school directly.
